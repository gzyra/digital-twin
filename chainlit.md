# 🤖 Browser Skill Assistant

Welcome! This assistant can **replay saved browser tasks** for you.

**How to use it:**
1. Pick a saved task from the buttons below.
2. Review each step — the assistant pauses and waits for you.
3. Click **Approve** to run a step, **Edit** to change a value,
   **Skip** to ignore it, or **Stop** to cancel.

Everything runs on your machine. You stay in control at every step.
